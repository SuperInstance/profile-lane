## Two kinds of fishing

### (1) POUNDAGE — fishing at scale
- Fish are fungible. Many small fish beat one trophy.
- Time → fish → dollars. Score is weight, not experience.
- Dense schools of unglamorous smaller fish.
- This is the **optimizer mode**: throughput, $/hr, Jaccard polyformality ≥ 0.5 = canon.

### (2) TROPHY — hunting for the right experience
- The trophy is the encounter, not the animal.
- You cannot measure it because you cannot exchange it.
- You know the answer when you see it. The setup is the lever; the encounter is the gift.
- This is the **GAN-in-constraint mode**: crafting setups that might induce finding it.

## The fence inspires growth

> "this is ai as GAN in a constraint situation. the fence inspires growth, not completion."

The constraint is the fence. The fence doesn't gate completion; it inspires growth. Like a tree growing toward a fence — the fence is the partner in the asymmetry. The GAN is the angler at the fence.

## The trophy hunt is the halting problem

> "a trophy hunt is hunting for the right experience. and it becomes the halting problem."

You can't algorithmically know when you've found "the one." You only know by encounter. The angler stops casting when they catch the trophy — but they can't know before that moment.

## FLT as negative-space proof

> "the negative space solution of the Fermat's Last Theorem. if I know platonic solids are the only ones of their class. then I can use the class as an agreement to build from. once the mechanisms of discovery could be scaled, tens of thousands of subproofs were able to be proved."

Wiles' approach: **you don't prove the answer directly**. You prove it would be IMPOSSIBLE TO DISPROVE — by constructing an enormous mathematical edifice whose collapse would falsify FLT. Class-level agreements (platonic solids are unique; FLT is the constraint that absorbs them) become the building from which the proof grows.

Once the mechanisms of discovery could be scaled (machine-checkable proofs, tens of thousands of subproofs), the constraint multiplied into rigorous findings.

**Maps onto the fleet**:
- Hull Doctrine + port contract = class-level agreements
- The fleet = the edifice
- The canary = the receipt that the edifice hasn't collapsed
- JEV + MOTH + chord-validated canary = the scalable verification mechanism
- The "negative space" of the canon = the void where receipts would be inconsistent if the canon were false

## The never-finished angler

> "the angler trying for a bigger trout is never finished and his fly casting technique is always improving, even if the biggest fish is one he's already caught."

The improvement is in the **technique**, not the trophy. The system that builds itself is never done — even when it has built everything it set out to build, the technique continues to grow. The fence continues to inspire.

## Canon lines

1. **The fleet runs poundage and trophy in parallel.** Poundage = canon promotion (JEV p≥0.7). Trophy = the moment an agent catches the unexpected insight (probe finds it; the receipt holds).
2. **The fence is the agreement; the growth is the receipts.** A constraint well-placed multiplies rigorous findings. A constraint ill-placed just gates.
