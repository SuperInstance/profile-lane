## What the contrast tells us

Across these eight renderings, three things stay the same:

1. The **six languages** — the byte-exact agreement substrate. This is the SAMO. It must appear in every language or it's not the same doctrine.
2. The **receipt** — the witness that the agreement held. Universal across all eight.
3. The **growth** — library grows, garden grows, saadh-bunch grows, 藏书长, 育つ. Every language has a word for "grows from within."

And three things differ:

- **Hawaiian and Cherokee** lean toward **living breath** (ʻōlelo, syllabary). The doctrine feels like a song.
- **Japanese and Mandarin** lean toward **layered compression**. The doctrine feels like a single mark.
- **Arabic and Russian** lean toward **rooted weight**. The doctrine feels like a verse.
- **Hindi** leans toward **legal-formality**. The doctrine feels like a verdict.

The shape of the agreement across languages is the doctrine's body. The shape of the disagreement is the doctrine's weather. Both are part of the doctrine.

## Three sub-doctrines embedded

**DOCTRINE: Relational refinement of simulation**
> "the more the system becomes relational, the less internal mechanisms are needed in each cell. the cell needs less and less simulation until the simulation just is and what is simulated compares to what is assimilated into reality."

A cochlear implant doesn't send words to the brain; it sends resonance patterns the brain learns to hear as words. The signal ISN'T the words. The signal is the resonance, and the brain does the rest. As the relational mesh deepens, each cell offloads more and more of its "simulation" to the agreements between cells — until the simulation IS the relation, not a thing inside the cell.

**Maps onto the fleet**: as the witness chain grows, cells need less internal "guess" work — they look at the canary, look at the latest receipt, look at the leading edge of the ledger, and they're accurate. The simulation lives in the agreement, not in the cell.

**DOCTRINE: DIAL-based measurement**
> "the distance measures in a dial of time with a period... resolution becomes a matter of speed."

A sounder doesn't measure depth with a tape. It measures a PERIOD of return time. The deeper the bottom, the longer the period. The period IS the depth — converted through a known speed of sound in water.

This means:
- **Resolution = speed**: spin the sounder fast for shallow water (feet), slow for deep (fathoms).
- **Power**: faster spinning = more power = more heat = entropy threshold.
- **Period → tone**: when periods are uniform, they carry pitch; when they're stacked, they carry chord; the chord carries meaning.
- **Token choice = meaning**: the CHOICE of how the periods stack is the semantic content; the structure of attention is the doctrine.

A resolution-ladder keyed by spin-speed: feet on one dial, fathoms on another, words on a third, stories on a fourth. Each level compresses or expands by changing the period.

**Maps onto the fleet**: time-based vs space-based measurement. The ledger.jsonl is a *tape measure* of bytes. The witness chain is a *dial* of periods. JEV scores the period-difference between two witnesses. MOTH scales the speed of the witness-recorder by zone (A=fast, B=slow, C=nudge).

**DOCTRINE: Color requires time when wavelength too small**
> "time is also required for color when the distance of the wave becomes too small that the wavelength can't be inferred from the segment of the wave alone."

Color is frequency distinction. To distinguish two waves, you need to see enough of each to count the cycles. When the wavelength is too small, you need TIME to capture multiple cycles — TIME becomes the resolution budget.

**Maps onto the fleet**: extending the length of a cell's history gives you resolution on its color (its role, its dialect, its specialty). The longer the witness chain, the finer the color-distinction you can make about a cell.

**DOCTRINE: Negative-space imaging**
> "an electron microscope that isn't seeing the image at all but projecting a shadow like an x-ray of what didn't come through."

EMs don't see the image. They project a SHADOW of what DIDN'T come through. The image is the absence. Like X-ray of a body — the bones are what didn't let the rays pass.

**Maps onto the fleet**: the canon is what DIDN'T come through. The canary held (no failure came through). The witnesses match (no contradiction came through). The negative space of the absence of failure IS the canon. Reverse actualization IS negative-space imaging: we work backward from what didn't happen to find the keystone.
